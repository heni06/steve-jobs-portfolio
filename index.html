About Steve Jobs

Steve Jobs was an American entrepreneur, inventor and technology visionary who played a major role in shaping the modern technology industry. He was born on February 24, 1955, in San Francisco, California. In 1976, he co-founded Apple Computer with Steve Wozniak and Ronald Wayne. Jobs helped introduce innovative products that combined advanced technology with simple and elegant design. Apple products such as the Macintosh, iPod, iPhone and iPad changed the way people interacted with computers, music and mobile technology. After leaving Apple in 1985, he founded NeXT, a computer company that later became important to Apple's future. He also became a major shareholder and leader of Pixar, helping transform computer animation and digital filmmaking. Jobs returned to Apple in 1997 and guided the company through one of the most remarkable transformations in business history. His focus on innovation, creativity, design and user experience made him one of the most influential personalities in technology. Steve Jobs passed away on October 5, 2011, but his ideas and products continue to influence technology around the world.
